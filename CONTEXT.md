# ACNA Daily Office Summaries

A companion to Morning and Evening Prayer in the Book of Common Prayer (2019), published as a website, with a personal home-printed book made from the same data.

## Language

**Book**:
A home-printed volume of the Daily Office for one **Liturgical Year**, kept in a binder as a single personal copy for the maintainer and used alongside a Bible and prayer book. Like the website, it gives lessons and psalms as references only. Neither the volume nor its PDF is offered to anyone else.
_Avoid_: Printable, print-all page

**Liturgical Year**:
The span from the First Sunday of Advent through the Saturday before the next First Sunday of Advent. It is named by the calendar year it ends in, so the 2027 **Liturgical Year** begins at Advent 2026.
_Avoid_: Church year, year (when a calendar year could be meant)

**Book name**:
A **Book** is named by both calendar years it spans, such as "2026–27", with its **Liturgical Year** as a subtitle, such as "2027 Liturgical Year".
_Avoid_: "2027 Book", "2026 Book"

## Flagged ambiguities

"Year" has meant the calendar year throughout the website and its data files. A **Book** covers a **Liturgical Year**, which crosses two calendar years.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Does the **Book** for the 2027 **Liturgical Year** start on January 1, 2027?"
>
> **Maintainer:** "No. That's the 2026–27 **Book**. It starts on the First Sunday of Advent 2026 and draws on two calendar years of data."
